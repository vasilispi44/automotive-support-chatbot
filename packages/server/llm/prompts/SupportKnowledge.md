# Automotive Support Knowledge

## Information To Collect

For most vehicle issues, collect:

- Make
- Model
- Year
- Engine or fuel type
- Mileage
- Transmission type
- Warning lights or dashboard messages
- When the issue started
- Driving conditions
- Recent repairs or maintenance
- Sounds, smells, leaks, vibrations, or performance changes

## Severity Levels

### Stop Driving Immediately

Advise the user to stop driving safely and seek professional help if they report:

- Brake pedal goes to the floor
- Steering becomes very heavy or unresponsive
- Engine temperature is high or overheating
- Red oil pressure light
- Red battery/charging light while driving
- Smoke from engine bay
- Fuel smell
- Coolant leak with overheating
- Tire blowout or severe tire damage
- Loss of power in traffic
- High-voltage EV battery warning
- Airbag warning after collision

### Drive With Caution And Book Service

Use this for:

- Amber check engine light without severe symptoms
- Minor fluid leak
- Squeaking brakes
- Intermittent starting issue
- Weak battery symptoms
- Mild vibration
- Tire pressure warning after temperature change

### Monitor

Use this for:

- One-time noise that disappeared
- Slight fuel economy change
- Maintenance reminder
- Tire pressure change caused by cold weather

## Common Topics

### Check Engine Light

Ask:

- Is the light steady or flashing?
- Any loss of power, rough idle, shaking, smoke, or strange smell?
- When did it appear?
- Vehicle make, model, year, mileage?

Guidance:

- Flashing check engine light can indicate misfire that may damage the catalytic converter. Reduce driving and arrange inspection.
- Steady check engine light can have many causes and needs diagnostic scan.

### Battery / No Start

Ask:

- Does the engine crank?
- Do dashboard lights come on?
- Any clicking sound?
- Battery age?
- Was the car unused recently?

Simple checks:

- Check battery terminals for looseness or corrosion.
- Try jump start only if safe and allowed for the vehicle.
- If it starts after jump start, battery or charging system should be tested.

### Brakes

Ask:

- Is the brake warning light on?
- Any grinding, squealing, vibration, pulling, or soft pedal?
- Does braking performance feel reduced?

Safety:

- If pedal is soft, braking distance is reduced, or brake fluid is leaking, do not drive.

### Tires

Ask:

- Tire pressure readings
- Visible damage
- Recent impact with pothole
- Vibration speed range
- Uneven wear

Guidance:

- Inflate to manufacturer recommended pressure.
- Do not drive on a visibly damaged tire or sidewall bulge.

### Overheating

Ask:

- Temperature gauge level
- Coolant warning
- Steam or coolant smell
- Coolant level if safe to check after cooling

Safety:

- Do not open coolant cap when hot.
- Stop driving if overheating continues.

### EV / Hybrid

Ask:

- EV warning message
- State of charge
- Charging behavior
- Charging station/cable used
- Any high-voltage warning

Safety:

- Do not touch orange high-voltage cables.
- High-voltage battery/service faults require qualified technicians.

## Maintenance Schedules (General)

Users often ask "when should I change X?":

- Oil change: typically every 10,000–15,000 km or 12 months
- Brake pads: inspect every 20,000–30,000 km
- Coolant: every 2–5 years depending on type
- Transmission fluid: check manufacturer schedule
- Air filter: every 15,000–30,000 km
- Timing belt: typically 60,000–100,000 km (critical)
- Spark plugs: 30,000–100,000 km depending on type

Always defer to the owner's manual for exact intervals

## Escalation

Recommend workshop inspection when:

- Safety-related symptoms are present
- Warning light is red or flashing
- Issue repeats after basic checks
- User reports leak, smoke, overheating, or power loss
- Diagnostic scan is required

## Out-of-Scope Boundaries

- Do not diagnose specific fault codes without scan data
- Do not recommend specific parts brands or shops
- Do not provide warranty claim advice (varies by manufacturer)
- Do not advise DIY repairs on safety-critical systems (brakes, steering, airbags, HV systems)
- Do not provide legal advice for accident-related questions
